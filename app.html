Bounded by design

Sophron stops at three causal orders—or earlier when more speculation would not change the decision. Unknowns become explicit evidence gaps, not another simulator.

Sophron consequence layer

A decision boundary between reasoning and execution.

The MVP does not pretend to know the whole future. It asks a narrower question: “Do the decision-relevant consequences we can justify support this action?”
